- **Decisions**: kept the palm on top of the base's thigh (around 0) because an earlier correction said "on top of the upper leg". That was wrong: the photo (Pose084) shows the hand wrapping the leg at the knee, and a sideways stabilising grip only works from the outside, pulling against the base's leg, which is exactly the Physics rule on grips. I read the photo for position along the leg, not for which side the hand presses. I also treated the 29° lower knee as an error to minimise ("photos show nearly straight legs") and tried to get it straighter; the human made it 40° instead and fixed the real oddity, the 35° hip rotation, plus ankle angles I never looked at. Lesson: a bent lower knee is how the ankle cross works; judge the leg by hip rotation and foot orientation, not by knee bend. Pose085 shows the flyer's back (facing the base's feet), so it is mirrored or a different variant; I followed Pose084 and the description.
