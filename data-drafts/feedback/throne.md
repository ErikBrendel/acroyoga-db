The base's feet sit a bit too far out on the flyer's thighs, too close to the knees. In the reference photos (Gieger Pose071, Acrodemy kulcsolt-tron500keret, yogaemotion) the base's soles are about the middle of the flyer's thighs, on the inner underside, and the flyer's knees stick out clearly beyond the base's feet. Move them a bit further in (about 50–55% along the thigh, turned a little more to the inside) and keep the instep hooks holding.
