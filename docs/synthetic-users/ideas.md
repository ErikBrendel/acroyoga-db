# Ideas for the product

## 2026-10-16, phase 1 (personas), from reading

### Tool helps
- Alternate and regional names on each pose, found by search ("floating paschi" / "teardrop", "ninja star" / "ninja bat") — Marco, Tobi, Lena
- Make your own flow from poses you know, keep it and share it as a link with one partner or a class — Mira, Marco
- "What's next" from a pose: its harder neighbours, for browsing ideas — Mira
- A one-screen or printable overview of beginner poses, for a class or a cheat sheet — Marco
- Browse poses by shape (thumbnails) when the name is unknown — Tobi, Lena
- State the scope (L-base acroyoga, 73 poses) up front; standing and hand-to-hand acro and the 1,500-skill libraries are a different product — Tobi

### Status quo wins
- Where and how to start as a beginner: experienced people at the jam or class do this better; at most a "go to a jam / class" pointer — Lena
- Which poses need a spotter, safety, adapting to an injury: learned from people who can see you; at most a short "use a spotter" reminder — Sanne

## 2026-10-17, phase 2 (stories), from reading

### Tool helps
- Build your own flow from the map and share it as one link ("this is what we did tonight", "our flow for Jonas") — Marco S1, Mira S3, Lena S4, Tobi S5; strongest gap, the dated flows in flows.json are already a hand-made class log
- Alternate names in search (floating paschi / teardrop, corkscrew / high barrel roll) — Lena S1, Mira S5, Tobi S3, Sanne S4
- Ambiguous names: one name used for different poses depending on the community ("bird" for front plank in some groups, "ninja star" / "ninja bat"); show how a name is used across communities instead of deciding which is right — Marco S4, Tobi S3, Mira S5. Status quo cannot do this: everyone knows only their local usage
- Recognise a pose by its shape (thumbnails, 3D) when the name differs or is forgotten — Lena S1, Sanne S4, Marco S3
- "What borders what we know, one step harder", as a progression view from a pose — Mira S1, Tobi S1, Lena S3; the graph is what AcroPassport, Jacob Brown and YouTube lack
- Per pose what it asks of the base's legs (one leg, which side); the merge-mirrored toggle hides exactly this difference — Sanne S1, S2
- Difficulty shown as a rough opinion with what leads into it, not as a level or permission — Marco S6, Tobi S4, Lena S5
- A large, one-hand "next step" view of a flow on the phone — Mira S4
- State the scope (L-base acroyoga) for visitors from standing / hand-to-hand acro — Tobi S6

### Status quo wins
- Finding partners and jams: Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram, classes — Lena S6
- Naming a move in the moment: someone answers within the hour, with context — Tobi S3, Mira S5, Marco S4
- Readiness, spotting, injuries: teachers and regulars who see you — Lena S5, Sanne S3, Tobi S4
- Set flows of a training or school: their own videos — Marco S7
- Performance and choreography — Mira S6
- Jam games: the most experienced person calls out poses; an app exists — Lena S7
- Remembering how a move feels: their own phone video beats a diagram; only names and order are missing — Mira S2
